# Story Before Technology

| Field | Value |

> People remember why something exists long after they have forgotten which framework I used.

Technology matters, but it is rarely the reason a project is interesting.

The site should first explain:

- what I noticed;
- who or what was affected;
- why the problem was worth exploring;
- what changed as I learned more.

Only then should it explain the implementation.

A technology should be prominent when the choice itself shaped the outcome, introduced an important trade-off, or enabled something that would otherwise have been impractical.

A technology badge is not a story.

## Test

A non-specialist hiring manager should understand why the work matters before reaching the architecture section.

A technical reader should still find enough depth to evaluate the decisions.
