A CV tells you what someone shipped. It does not tell you how they decided what to ship,
what they got wrong on the way, or what they would do differently now.

That second set of answers is the interesting one, and it is the one that is almost never
written down. So this site is an attempt to write it down: the question that started each
project, the approach I took, the parts that failed, and what I learned.

The rule I have set myself is simple. Every entry has to include something that did not
work. If a write-up reads like a success story, it is not finished.
